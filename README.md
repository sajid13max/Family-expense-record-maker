# 💰 My Money Manager — Family Expense Tracker

**My Money Manager** is a modern and stylish family expense-tracking web app designed to help families manage their monthly finances in one organized place. It makes it easy to record, monitor, and understand where the family’s money is being spent.

### ✨ Key Features

* 📊 Monthly expense tracking
* 💵 Track income and spending
* 🧾 Add and manage individual expenses
* 🏷️ Organize expenses by category
* 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Designed for family financial management
* 📅 View spending throughout the month
* 📈 Monthly financial summaries
* 💡 Quickly understand spending habits
* 📱 Fully responsive for phones, tablets, and desktops
* 🎨 Clean, modern, and user-friendly interface
* ⚡ Fast and lightweight

### 🎯 Purpose

The goal of **My Money Manager** is to give families a simple way to stay aware of their monthly expenses, control unnecessary spending, and build better financial habits.

Whether it's groceries, bills, transportation, education, shopping, entertainment, or other household costs, everything can be organized and tracked in one place.
